I am a M.S. student in the School of Electronic Science and Technology at **Nanjing University (NJU)**. I am fortunate to be co-advised by [Prof. Zhou](https://zhouyou-nju.github.io/) and [Prof. Cao](https://cite.nju.edu.cn/People/Faculty/20190621/i5054.html). I received my B.S. in Electronic Science and Technology from NJU (2020-2024).

My current research centers on developing **efficient and high-fidelity microscopy imaging methods**. I am passionate about bridging advanced algorithms and novel optical encoding techniques to overcome fundamental imaging challenges.

<!-- update each cycle stage -->
I plan to apply for Fall 2027 Ph.D. programs. I am particularly interested in **optical-computational co-design** for biomedical imaging, compact 3D sensing, and computational display systems.
